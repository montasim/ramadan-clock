# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Ramadan Clock contributors and maintainers are expected to create a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free environment. Be considerate, accept constructive feedback, focus criticism on ideas and code, and respect differences in background, identity, experience, and religious practice.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/montasim). Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.
